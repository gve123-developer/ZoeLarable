💡 Suggested Defense Talking Point:
"Over our 23-calendar-day historical window (July 10 to August 1, 2026), both algorithms demonstrated comparable forecasting error rates, with the 3-day SMA yielding an MAE of 0.52 compared to 0.54 for Exponential Smoothing (α = 0.7). Because of the short timeframe and intermittent zero-demand days, the hypothesis that Exponential Smoothing yields a lower MAE was not supported. However, both models successfully provided functional, non-negative restocking suggestions for the proprietors without requiring complex historical infrastructure."
